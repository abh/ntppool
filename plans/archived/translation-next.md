# Translation Priority: Next Languages to Add

## Analysis Summary

Based on 2024 internet user statistics by country and primary languages used, this analysis identifies the highest-impact missing languages for the NTP Pool project.

## Current Language Support

The NTP Pool currently supports 32 languages as defined in `i18n/languages.json`:
- European: German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, Polish, Czech, Hungarian, Romanian, Bulgarian, Greek, Croatian, Serbian, Ukrainian, Catalan, Euskera
- Asian: Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Vietnamese, Persian/Farsi, Hebrew, Arabic, Indonesian, Kazakh, Sinhala
- Note: Croatian and Chinese Traditional are marked as "testing"

## Missing Languages by Internet Users (2024)

### Top 10 Highest Impact Missing Languages

1. **Bengali (bn)**: ~276 million internet users
   - Bangladesh: 126.2 million users
   - Eastern India: ~150 million users
   - One of the most spoken languages globally with significant internet growth

2. **Urdu (ur)**: ~200+ million internet users
   - Pakistan: 170 million users
   - India: Additional Urdu speakers
   - Major language in South Asia

3. **Portuguese (Brazil) (pt-BR)**: ~165 million internet users
   - Brazil: 165.3 million users
   - Distinct from European Portuguese already supported
   - Brazil represents 200+ of 236 million global Portuguese speakers

4. **Tamil (ta)**: ~75 million internet users
   - Tamil Nadu, India + Sri Lanka + Singapore diaspora
   - Strong digital presence in South India

5. **Telugu (te)**: ~70 million internet users
   - Andhra Pradesh, India
   - Major Indian regional language

6. **Thai (th)**: ~61 million internet users
   - Thailand: 61.2 million users
   - Major Southeast Asian market with high digital adoption

7. **Punjabi (pa)**: ~50 million internet users
   - Punjab regions of India and Pakistan
   - Growing internet usage

8. **Gujarati (gu)**: ~45 million internet users
   - Gujarat, India + global diaspora
   - Significant business community online

9. **Marathi (mr)**: ~40 million internet users
   - Maharashtra, India
   - Strong regional internet presence

10. **Malay (ms)**: ~35 million internet users
    - Malaysia, Brunei
    - Note: Indonesian (id) is already supported

### Phase 3: Community Completion (170+ million users)
- **Punjabi (pa)** - 50 million users

## Total Impact

Adding these 10 languages would serve approximately **1.016+ billion additional internet users** who currently don't have the NTP Pool available in their primary language.

## Key Insights

- **South Asian languages dominate**: 7 of the top 10 missing languages are from the Indian subcontinent
- **Bengali has the highest impact**: 276 million users across Bangladesh and Eastern India
- **Brazilian Portuguese distinction**: While Portuguese exists, Brazilian Portuguese represents 84% of Portuguese speakers
- **Southeast Asian gap**: Thai and Malay represent significant regional opportunities

## Methodology

This analysis was based on:
1. 2024 internet user statistics by country from Statista and other sources
2. Primary language usage in each country
3. Current language support in `i18n/languages.json`
4. Internet penetration rates and digital adoption patterns
5. Correction: Indonesian (id) is already supported, so Malay (ms) is separate
